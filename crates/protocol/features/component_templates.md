Template directives of a component's definition, and named slots on a use (decision
`element-roles-and-reuse`): `core://Each` repeats a piece once per entry of a list argument,
`core://Match` keeps the piece whose key an argument or a binding names, and `core://Children` with
`slot` takes the nodes a use passes under `slots`. All three expand to plumbing a renderer already
draws. The engine expands components, so it must know them to read such a definition or such a use; the
pod records this feature on the element that has one.
