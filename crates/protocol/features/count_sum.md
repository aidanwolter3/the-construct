`core://Text` `count` with `sum: "<field>"` adds up that field over the counted entities (the same `query_key` and
`where`): `$sum_money` is the total as a price (`$518.84`, `$98`, `$1,299.95`), `$sum` as a plain number. Values that
aren't numbers count as 0. A bag's total, a week's minutes.

Fields: sum?.
