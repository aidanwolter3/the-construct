`core://Progress` with `value` and `shape: Ring` is a ring instead of a bar: a circle `size_dp` across (default 56),
its track quiet and `value / max` of it drawn clockwise from the top in `color`, with `label` (bound text, e.g.
`"$count/5"` or `"$item.done/$item.lessons"`) in the middle. A week's goal, a track's lessons.

Fields: shape (Bar | Ring), label?.
